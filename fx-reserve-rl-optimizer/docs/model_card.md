# Model card

## Intended use

Educational and research demonstration of constrained dynamic asset allocation for a stylised FX reserve portfolio.

## Not intended for

- Real trading
- Investment advice
- Actual central-bank reserve management
- Production risk systems

## Data

Public ETF/currency proxies or deterministic synthetic fallback data.

## Main risks

- Overfitting
- Non-stationarity
- Proxy-data limitations
- Reward misspecification
- Transaction-cost simplification
- No liquidity order-book modelling
