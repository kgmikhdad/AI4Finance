# Limitations

- Public ETF/currency proxies are not the same as actual central-bank reserve instruments.
- Transaction costs are simplified.
- The model does not include settlement, custody, liquidity microstructure, or regulatory accounting constraints.
- PPO training can overfit historical regimes.
- The reward function is stylised and should be interpreted as a research design, not a policy rule.
- Stress tests are simple one-day shocks, not full macro-financial scenario simulations.
